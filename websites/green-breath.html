Our Journey

From atmospheric problem to biological product

Every Green Breath system follows the same arc — from a global emissions challenge to a living, revenue-generating solution.

Problem

Rising atmospheric CO₂ is warming the planet faster than conventional capture methods can offset — and most solutions store carbon instead of putting it to work.

Research

Years of strain selection and photobioreactor design identify microalgae species that fix carbon at industrial efficiency in real-world climates.

Innovation

AI-monitored cultivation systems keep pH, light and nutrient dosing precisely tuned, turning biology into a predictable industrial process.

Products

Harvested biomass becomes biofertilizer, animal feed, natural pigments and cosmetic ingredients — carbon captured, then converted to value.

Future

Public installations, home bioreactors and biorefineries scale together into a distributed carbon economy, one reactor at a time.

    The science behind the system

    Short, clear explainers on the biology and climate concepts that power Green Breath.

    What are Microalgae?

    Microscopic aquatic organisms that photosynthesize up to ten times faster than land plants.

    Photosynthesis

    The process of converting light, water and CO₂ into biomass and oxygen — nature's original carbon capture.

    Carbon Capture

    Techniques that remove CO₂ from the air, ranging from mechanical filters to living biological systems.

    Circular Economy

    A model where waste from one process becomes the raw material for the next, minimizing loss.

    Hydroponics

    Growing plants in nutrient-rich water instead of soil, often paired with algae systems for shared resources.

    Climate Change

    Long-term shifts in temperature and weather patterns, driven largely by rising greenhouse gas concentrations.
